The Growth Algorithm

The "Double Progression" Rule

Stop guessing. Follow this loop for every single exercise.

Methodology: Volumetric Overload
1

Pick Your Weight

Select a weight where you can hit the Bottom of the Range (e.g., 8 reps) with perfect form, leaving 1-2 reps in the tank.

2

Earn the Reps

Keep the same weight every week. Your only goal is to add reps until you hit the Top of the Range (e.g., 12 reps) for ALL sets.

3

Level Up

Once you hit top reps on all sets, add the smallest plate (1.25kg - 2.5kg). Your reps will drop back to the bottom. Repeat Step 1.

FORM FAILURE = NO COUNT If you swing your body, the rep is 0.
LOG OR STAY SMALL Use 'Hevy' App or a Notebook.

Stuck / Plateaued?

If you can't add a rep for 2 weeks straight:
1. Check sleep/food.
2. If good, Drop weight by 10% and rebuild form.

The "BD Gym" Problem

Bangladeshi gyms often lack small plates. Bring your own 0.5kg water bottles to hang on the bar if the jump from 5kg to 10kg is too heavy.
