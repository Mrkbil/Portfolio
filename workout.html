The Growth Algorithm

The "Double Progression" Rule

Our standardized progress loop applied directly to your training.

Methodology: Volumetric Overload
1

Pick Your Weight

Select a load where you can execute the Bottom of the Range (e.g., 6 reps on OHP) with controlled form, leaving 1-2 reps in reserve.

2

Earn the Reps

Keep the same weight every week. Your primary target is to increase reps until you reach the Top of the Range (e.g., 8 reps) for all sets.

3

Level Up

Once top range reps are secure on all sets, add the smallest increment (1.25kg - 2.5kg). Your reps will drop to the bottom. Repeat Step 1.

FORM DEVIATION = INVALID REP Swaying, swinging, or compensatory movements void the repetition.
RECORD YOUR LOGS Ensure progress metrics are cataloged in an app or physical journal.

Plateau Guidelines

If you fail to add repetitions or load for 2-3 consecutive weeks:
1. Evaluate sleep and dietary targets.
2. If satisfactory, reduce working weight load by 10% and rebuild movement tempo.

Local Gym Adaptations

Local gyms often lack micro-plates. You can carry lightweight hanging ankle straps or pre-measured bottles to hook over bars to make small 0.5kg increments.
