🍳 Cooking & Preparation Guidelines

  • Rice: Boil 1–2 cups per meal. Optional 1 tsp ghee for flavor. Avoid frying in oil.
  • Roti: Whole wheat (atta). Roll thin, cook on non-stick tawa. Minimal oil if needed.
  • Chicken: Grill, bake, or curry with < 2 tsp oil. Avoid creamy sauces.
  • Eggs: Boil or omelette with minimal oil. Avoid deep frying.
  • Dal (Lentils/Chola): Boil until soft. Add minimal ghee or oil. Add seasonal veggies.
  • Vegetables: Steam, sauté lightly, or eat raw. Avoid deep frying.
  • Oats: Cook with water or low-fat milk. Add fruits or 1 tsp honey for taste.
  • Nuts (Almonds, Peanuts): Soak overnight. Eat raw or lightly roasted.
  • Pre/Post Workout: Banana + boiled chickpeas. Optional low-fat milk post-workout. Creatine 5g in water.
  • General Oil/Fat: Use < 1–2 tsp per cooking if needed. Prefer ghee, olive oil, or mustard oil for flavor.

🚫 Foods to Avoid (Priority)

  1. Sugary drinks: Soft drinks, sweetened juice, energy drinks – high empty calories.
  2. Fried foods: Samosa, puri, pakora, french fries – high in trans fats.
  3. Processed snacks: Chips, biscuits, packaged cakes – high in sugar and refined carbs.
  4. Excess oil: Avoid deep frying or using large amounts of oil in cooking.
  5. High-fat dairy: Cream, butter in large amounts, full-fat milk – adds unnecessary fat.
  6. Refined carbs: White bread, white pasta, instant noodles – low satiety, spike insulin.
  7. Fast food: Burger, pizza, fried chicken – high calories, low nutrition.
  8. Alcohol & sweets: Cakes, ice cream, chocolate bars – sugar + fat = belly fat.

⚡ Tip: Prioritize lean protein, complex carbs, fiber-rich veggies, and healthy fats to support 6-pack abs.
