# SCIENCE-BASED TRAINING PRINCIPLES

☑ Progressive overload is mandatory

☑ Most compounds stop 1-2 reps before failure

☑ Isolation exercises may reach failure on final set

☑ Recovery drives growth

☑ More volume is not always better

☑ Fatigue must be managed

☑ Weekly frequency around 2x per muscle

☑ Core trained hard enough to grow but not so often that recovery suffers

☑ Grip should not limit back development

☑ Exercise selection should have a clear purpose

☑ Every exercise must solve a specific goal

☑ Avoid redundant exercises with overlapping stimulus
