PHASE 0

Mathematical Foundations & Information Theory

Build raw mathematical machinery to derive gradient updates, prove convergence, and understand representations.

0/0
0.1 AI-Relevant Calculus (Anton)
  • Functions & Graphs, Limits & Continuity, Derivatives & Chain Rule
  • Partial Derivatives ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Highest Priority)
  • Gradient Vectors & Directional Derivatives
  • Jacobian Matrices (Vector-valued derivatives)
  • Hessian Matrices (Second-order optimization)
  • Multivariable Optimization (Critical points, saddle points)
  • Lagrange Multipliers (Constrained optimization)
  • Multiple Integrals (Basic understanding for expectations)
Skip: Advanced Integration tricks, Polar Coordinates, Infinite Series, Complex Analysis, Differential Equations.
0.2 Linear & Tensor Algebra
  • Vectors, Norms, Dot Products, Vector Spaces, Subspaces, Orthogonality
  • Matrices, Matrix Multiplication, Transpose, Trace, Rank, Determinant
  • Basis, Span, Linear Independence, Dimension
  • Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors, Eigendecomposition
  • Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
  • Matrix Calculus (Derivatives of matrix expressions, vector gradients)
  • Kronecker Product, Tensor Algebra, Matrix Factorization Methods
0.3 Probability, Statistics & Inequalities
  • Probability Axioms, Rules, Conditional Probability, Bayes' Theorem
  • Random Variables, Discrete & Continuous Distributions (Gaussian, Bernoulli, Poisson, Uniform)
  • Expectation, Variance, Covariance, Joint & Marginal Distributions
  • Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) & Maximum A Posteriori (MAP)
  • Hypothesis Testing, p-values, Confidence Intervals
  • Law of Large Numbers (LLN) & Central Limit Theorem (CLT)
  • Probability Inequalities: Jensen's, Markov, Chebyshev Inequalities
0.4 Information Theory
  • Shannon Entropy & Differential Entropy
  • Cross-Entropy Loss (Derivation from MLE)
  • Mutual Information (MI)
  • Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence & Jensen-Shannon (JS) Divergence
  • Maximum Entropy Principle
0.5 Optimization Theory (Convex Optimization — Boyd)
  • Convex Sets & Convex Functions
  • Duality Theory, Primal/Dual Formulations, Lagrange Dual Function
  • Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) Conditions
  • Deisenroth MML (Chapters 1–12 Complete)
