How to Coach His Starting Weights

Never prescribe arbitrary loads like "squat 50kg." Let his capacity dictate the load.

Dynamic Load Calibration
Clean Reps & ~3 RIR (Correct)

If prescribed 3 × 8–12 and he hits:

Set 1: 10 | Set 2: 10 | Set 3: 9

Form was crisp and he had ~3 reps left. Keep this starting weight and apply double progression.

Too Heavy (Cut Load)

If he attempts the weight and gets:

Set 1: 5 | Set 2: 4 | Set 3: 4

Weight is too heavy for a beginner's connective tissues. Drop load by 15–20% immediately.

Too Light (Step Up)

If he breezes through easily and gets:

Set 1: 12 | Set 2: 12 | Set 3: 12 (+8 easy)

Zero mechanical challenge. Safely increase dumbbell or machine pin load for next session.

The "Double Progression" Rule (E.g. Incline DB Press 3 × 8–12)

1. First Baseline
12.5–15 kg × 10, 9, 8
Hits safe bottom range
2. Earn Reps
15 kg × 10, 10, 9
Same weight, add repetitions
3. Ceiling Reached
15 kg × 12, 12, 12
Top of rep target earned
4. Level Up
17.5 kg × 8–10
Smallest jump; repeat cycle

Aerobic & Endurance Progression

Avoid aggressive, exhausting cardio out of the gate. Build aerobic capacity gradually.

Weeks 1–2: 2 cardio sessions × 15–20 minutes (low-to-moderate steady state) + regular daily steps.
Weeks 3–4: 2 cardio sessions × 20–30 minutes (incline walking, stationary cycle, or elliptical).

*Target intensity: Conversational pace. Breathing harder, but able to speak in short sentences without gasping.
